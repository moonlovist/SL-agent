# Arc Morphology Knowledge

- Observable arc properties include curvature proxy, elongation, angular extent, continuity, radius, tangential misalignment, and S/N stability.
- High S/N proves detectability, not lens origin.
- A tangential elongated structure separated from the inner galaxy is more lens-specific than a generic bright residual.
- Connection to the central disk weakens a detached-image interpretation and motivates an intrinsic-structure test.
- Fragmentation can be physical, noise-driven, or caused by deblending; stability across thresholds is relevant but not definitive.
