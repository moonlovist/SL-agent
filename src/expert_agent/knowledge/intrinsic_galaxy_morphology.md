# Intrinsic Galaxy Morphology Knowledge

- Spiral arms and rings may be curved, blue, clumpy, and approximately centred on a galaxy.
- Physical continuity with the disk, repeated arm-like winding, or alignment with the galaxy plane supports an intrinsic explanation.
- A ring may imitate an Einstein ring; colour contrast and shared lens geometry alone do not fully separate them.
- Reliable disk morphology and multi-band colour are often missing from a single-band cutout, so absence of evidence must remain uncertainty.
