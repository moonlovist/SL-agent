# Artifact and Subtraction Knowledge

- Foreground-model mismatch can leave rings, positive/negative lobes, central residuals, or ellipticity-aligned structures.
- PSF mismatch can create compact or radially patterned residuals near a bright core.
- Bad pixels, edges, cosmic rays, and correlated noise can survive a simple S/N threshold.
- Stability across extraction settings supports robustness of detection, but a stable modelling residual may still be non-astrophysical.
- Original image, model, residual, RMS map, and PSF context should be considered together when available.
