# Colour and Multi-band Knowledge

- A colour difference between central galaxy and surrounding structure can support distinct source populations.
- Blue arcs around a red galaxy are common lens candidates but are not unique to lensing; star-forming rings and arms can be blue.
- Comparable colours among multiple images can support a shared background source, subject to dust, PSF, and photometric uncertainties.
- At low signal-to-noise ratio, apparent colour differences or agreements may be unreliable. Any color conclusion must retain its measurement uncertainty and the available-band information.
- Single-band data cannot measure colour. The agent must mark colour evidence unavailable rather than infer it from intensity.
