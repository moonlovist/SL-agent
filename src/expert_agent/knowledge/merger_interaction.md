# Merger and Interaction Knowledge

- Multiple cores, asymmetric tidal features, bridges, tails, and irregular extended light support interaction or merger explanations.
- Compact companions can be confused with multiple lensed images without colour/redshift information.
- A merger may coexist with lensing; this hypothesis is an alternative explanation for the observed morphology, not a claim of mutual exclusivity.
- Failure to measure multiple cores or tidal asymmetry is missing evidence unless the data were demonstrably sensitive to them.
