# Observation Audit Skill

## Purpose
Judge whether an Observation document is fit for reasoning. Do not infer a lens label.

## Procedure
1. Check pixel validity and positive noise estimates.
2. Check foreground-subtraction status and measurement reliability.
3. Verify every structure points to known measurements.
4. Record missing RMS/PSF context as limitations rather than invented facts.
5. Mark blocking failures as unusable; otherwise preserve warnings as limited quality.

## Guardrail
Image quality and structure detectability are not evidence that gravitational lensing occurred.
